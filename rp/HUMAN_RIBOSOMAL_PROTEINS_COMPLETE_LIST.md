## Important Notes

### Unified Nomenclature
The unified nomenclature (Ban et al., 2014; Current Opinion in Structural Biology 2014) uses prefixes:
- **uS/uL**: Universal proteins (conserved across bacteria, archaea, eukaryotes)
- **eS/eL**: Eukaryote-specific proteins
- **S/L**: Small or Large subunit

### Special Cases

1. **Ubiquitin Fusion Proteins**:
   - **RPS27A** (eS31): Encoded as ubiquitin-eS31 fusion, gene symbol RPS27A (UniProt: P62979)
   - **RPS30** (eS30): Part of FAU gene encoding FUBI-eS30 fusion (UniProt: P62861)
   - **RPL40** (eL40): Part of UBA52 gene encoding ubiquitin-eL40 fusion (UniProt: P62987)

2. **Sex Chromosome Variants**:
   - **RPS4X** (eS4): X chromosome variant (UniProt: P62701)
   - **RPS4Y1** (eS4): Y chromosome variant 1 (UniProt: P22090)
   - **RPS4Y2** (eS4): Y chromosome variant 2 (UniProt: Q8TD47)

3. **Non-ribosomal Ribosome Component**:
   - **RACK1**: Receptor for activated C kinase 1, part of 40S subunit (UniProt: P63244)

4. **Paralogous Proteins**:
   - **RPL22L1**: eL22-like paralog (UniProt: Q6P5R6)
   - **RPL26L1**: uL24-like paralog (UniProt: Q9UNX3)

5. **Nomenclature Note**:
   - **RPL41** (eL41): Recently renamed to **eS32** as it associates with the small subunit
